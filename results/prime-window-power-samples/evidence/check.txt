Scope of the Lean statements

The square and doubled-prime theorems use just their stated arithmetic
sample hypotheses. Their conclusions are Mathlib's RiemannHypothesis.
The fixed-power transfer also assumes the increment envelope, the upper
bound for zero real parts and the supremum property. The paper derives
these for the actual prime sum and zeta zeros. It supplies a second
interpolation argument via partial summation and proves the ordinary-prime
forward implication. Those analytic steps and the quantitative
every-block corollary and unconditional consecutive-run theorem have written proofs.
