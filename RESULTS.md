On fourth-power samples, a logarithmic bound for the negative part of a paired Möbius sum on arbitrarily late blocks is equivalent to RH. The finite certificate bounds the loss by $2(1+2\log m)$ for every $10{,}000\le m\le2{,}000{,}000$. A companion proves that a weighted sum over the zeros right of the critical line is nonnegative on a terminal interval of arbitrarily late square blocks, and leaves the full-interval bound open. [Möbius criterion and certificates](results/prime-window-power-samples/pairs/mobius/) · [Signed cutoffs](results/prime-window-power-samples/pairs/mobius/CUTOFFS.md)

The power-sampling result now also gives an $8/9$ upper bound for the mean square on every complete short block through $K=4\cdot10^{24}$, using OpenAI's Lean theorem for the zero-free half-plane $\Re s>7/8$. [Finite theorem and certificate](results/prime-window-power-samples/pairs/ODD-MEAN-SQUARE.md) [Release with both calibration checks](https://github.com/leecrossley/prime-window-rh-criterion/releases/tag/odd-shift-mean-square-v1.0.2)

The [square-sample median and pair-discrepancy results](results/prime-window-power-samples/square-blocks/) give an RH equivalence on shrinking blocks, a finite zero-exception range and a sharp lower limit for a weighted pair statistic. ## Geometric proof-engineering note

The rank-three companion identifies Mizusawa’s genus field with the auxiliary field in Lean. Given his 2010 rank theorem, the dyadic window has at most three exceptional indices and its dependence on inverse precision is cubic. The package includes a control using the same theorem at nine. A separate Mathlib-only proof shows that the primitive root of unity is a nonsquare at all six primes above the auxiliary pair in each full dyadic cyclotomic layer of conductor at least 16. [Rank-three result and replay](results/geometric-window-descent/mizusawa/README.md)

This note’s version of Sanftenberg’s ordinary-character specialisation of Alpöge’s announced Lean proof lowers the generator bound for the prime-two tower from 24 to nine. Mizusawa proved in 2010 that its ordinary class module is free of rank three over the 2-adic integers. The Lean proof also uses the second automorphy case directly in the rank-four count and reaches Mathlib’s RH through the geometric argument. A direct arithmetic bound for the signed sum on arbitrarily late complete blocks remains open. [Mizusawa, Lemma 4.2](https://jtnb.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/jtnb.707.pdf#page=13) [Note and prior work](results/geometric-window-descent/) [Lean source](results/geometric-window-descent/lean/SecondSeedRH.lean) [Replay and comparison](results/geometric-window-descent/REPLAY.md)

## Checking the collection

When a collection file changes, stage new paths with Git, then run `python3 tools/collection_manifest.py --write` to refresh `COLLECTION.sha256`.

Run `sh verify-collection.sh` from the repository root to check the collection files and the manifests for every result directory. The index must point to an existing result with a manifest. Each result’s README gives its mathematical replay and environment requirements. [Contribution and publication guidance](AGENTS.md)
